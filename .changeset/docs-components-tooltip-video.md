Two new docs components. **Tooltip** puts a short note on a word or phrase in a sentence (`<Tooltip note="Hot module replacement">HMR</Tooltip>`). It opens on hover and keyboard focus, and on tap on a touch screen; a click keeps it open, and Escape closes it. **Video** plays a video file with native controls, a `poster` and a `.vtt` `captions` track. `loop` plays a clip like a GIF: muted, looping, with no controls and a pause button. Under reduced motion it waits on its poster until the reader presses play. Given a YouTube watch, short, Shorts or embed link, it shows the thumbnail and loads the player from `youtube-nocookie.com` only when the reader presses play. See the new Tooltip and Video pages.
