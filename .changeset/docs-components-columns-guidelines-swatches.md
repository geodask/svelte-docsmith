Three new docs components for design-system docs. **Columns** sets content side by side in two or three columns (`<Columns cols={3}>`), stacking into one on phones. **Do** and **Dont** are panels for guidelines, with a coloured top edge and a "Do" or "Don't" label in words and an icon; their colours are the new `--docsmith-do` and `--docsmith-dont` theme tokens. **ColorSwatch** shows any CSS colour with its name and the value it resolves to in the current colour mode (`<ColorSwatch value="var(--brand)" />`), and **ColorSwatches** lays them out in a grid. See the new Columns, Do & Don't and Colour swatches pages.
